Looping Crosswords

What is a Looping Crossword?

In a traditional crossword, each word in the grid is bounded by either a wall or the edge of the grid.

A looping crossword relaxes this restriction: words will loop over the edge of the grid to the other side!

The particulars of the looping pattern are defined per-puzzle.
